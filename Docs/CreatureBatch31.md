# Blue jelly, spotted jelly and ochre jelly

Batch 31 follows the handoff queue. All three are hostile, neuter, mindless,
breathless, amorphous, eyeless, headless and limbless, unable to take objects.
Official sex pairs are identical: blue 116/117, spotted 118/119, ochre 120/121.
Wizard sex requests still display neuter glyphs. Synthetic sex aliases verify

Pinned include/monsters.h: blue is level 4/difficulty 5, immobile, with passive
cold contact and cold/poison resistance. Spotted is level 5/difficulty 6,
immobile, with passive acid and acid/stone resistance. Ochre is level 6/
difficulty 8, moving, with acid engulf/contact and acid/stone resistance.
Gameplay, speed, resistances, attack and perception rules are unchanged.

Actual default tiles guide blue/cyan, blue/cyan with orange spots, and ochre/
orange/red with peach upper accents. Lime marks are irregular gel inclusions,
not reconstructed eyes; no pupils or faces are added. Exterior black shadow
fringe is spatially excluded while enclosed details remain in comparisons.
Masks are approximate diagnostics. Source tiles stay unmodified.

Independent width, depth, height, base flow and upright fold proportions create
three continuous soft volumes. Spotted jelly has asymmetric folds and orange
spots painted onto its body, never raised spot geometry. Blue is broader/lower;
ochre has the tallest central fold and a broad flowing base. Initial folds
were too subtle in studio review; final generation raises them for readability.
These are static gel folds, not heads, appendages or anatomy claims. Exact
composition, hidden surface/underside, inclusion placement and pose are art.

Existing M_Gel is reused unchanged: opaque vertex colour and .32 roughness.
No transparency, swallowed inventory, attack effect, animation, fur or rig.
